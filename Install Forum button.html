Forum contributions button

A one-click button for the grader view in Forum. It lists every student speaking turn in a class. Click one and the class video jumps to that moment.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar Press Cmd + Shift + B (Chrome and Safari).
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar Forum contributions

    Can't drag it? Right-click the button, choose "Copy Link", then add a new bookmark and paste the link as its address.

  3. Use it in Forum Open a class, go to Assess Class → Video, and click the bookmark. A panel appears. Click any turn to jump there. Drag the panel by its title to move it, or click the bookmark again to close it.
  4. Want transcripts? In the panel, click Save for transcription, then double-click Transcribe class in the forum-contribution-checker folder. The first run sets itself up, which takes a few minutes.

The button runs in your own browser with your Forum login and only reads the class's event log (who spoke when). Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. If clicking it does nothing in Safari, try Chrome.
